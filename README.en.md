## ⚙️ How does Xbox-FTP-Transfer work?

**Xbox-FTP-Transfer** was designed inside AndroidIDE to perform robust and direct FTP transfers from your Android phone to the Xbox 360 (RGH/JTAG), optimizing how you manage and install your games.

The app goes far beyond a common FTP client, featuring a smart logic tailored specifically for the Xbox file structure.

### 🚀 Key Features

* **Optimized Game Transfer:** Full support for selecting and uploading game folders in **GOD** and **XEX** formats.
* **Metadata Intelligence:** Automatic identification of **Title IDs** and recursive search for game executables out-of-the-box.
* **Visual Management:** Automatically downloads and displays game covers directly on your phone using a caching system, alongside an automatic cataloging system for games already installed on the console.
* **Console Optimization:** The app creates identification markers directly within the Xbox storage, which speeds up and optimizes future scans by your dashboard (such as Aurora).
* **Interface & Monitoring:** Full progress tracking with real-time notifications on your phone's screen, including audio alerts when the transfer is successfully completed.

### 🕹️ How to Use:

1. Make sure both your Xbox and your phone are connected to the same Wi-Fi network.
2. Enter the console's local IP address into the app.
   > [!NOTE]  
   > The default **username and password** is usually **xbox**.
3. Select the game folder (GOD or XEX) from your Android storage.
4. Start the transfer and track the progress and game covers being managed by the system in real time until you hear the completion audio alert!
