Privacy Policy
​Last Updated: Fri 3, 2026
​This Privacy Policy describes how the FTP Transfer application handles your device's permissions and data. The core focus of this application is the transparency and security of your information.
​
1. Requested Permissions and Their Purposes
​To ensure all game manager features work properly, the application requests the following permissions:
​Storage Access (Files/Media): Used to allow you to select game files (GOD or XEX formats) on your mobile device for transfer.
​Network / Wi-Fi Access (Network Connections): Required to establish direct communication via FTP protocol between your Android device and your Xbox 360 over the local area network (LAN).
​Internet Access: This permission is used exclusively to communicate with external metadata servers to download game covers and icons.
​
2. Data and Metadata Handling
​The application handles information in a hybrid manner to ensure efficiency:
​Offline Processing: The identification of Title IDs and the parsing of game folder structures are performed entirely locally on your device. This information is never sent to external servers.
​Content Downloading: To display game artwork, the app uses the locally identified Title ID to fetch covers and icons from the internet. These images are stored in a local cache on your device to enable faster access in the future.
​Data Privacy: FTP Transfer does not collect, store, or share any personal data, usage logs, or sensitive information with third parties. Its internet usage is strictly limited to retrieving visual artwork for your games.
​
3. Changes to this Policy
​This policy may be updated if new features are added to the application. We recommend reviewing this page periodically to stay informed.
